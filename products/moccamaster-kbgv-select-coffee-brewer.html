About This Product

The Moccamaster KBGV Select brews coffee the way the Specialty Coffee Association says it should be done — and it's certified to prove it. The copper boiling element heats water to the optimal 196-205°F range. It brews a full pot in 6 minutes and keeps it warm without burning on a hot plate. Handmade in the Netherlands with a 5-year warranty.
