How to Choose the Right Home Essentials

Five principles our stylists swear by when outfitting a space

1

Measure twice, buy once

Before falling in love with that stand mixer or air purifier, measure your counter space and clearance. A gorgeous appliance that lives in the pantry because it doesn't fit under your cabinets is money wasted.

2

Think about your actual routine

Buy for the life you live, not the one you imagine. If you drink coffee every morning, a quality espresso machine pays for itself in months. If you host once a year, skip the 12-quart Dutch oven.

3

Read the one-star reviews

Five-star reviews tell you what's great. One-star reviews tell you what breaks and what annoys people six months in. Look for patterns — a single complaint is an outlier, ten of the same complaint is a red flag.

4

Prioritize easy maintenance

The best product is the one you'll actually keep using. Dishwasher-safe parts, replaceable filters, and accessible customer support matter more than a long feature list.

5

Invest in daily-use items first

Spend more on things you touch every day — your vacuum, your coffee maker, your cookware. Decorative items and occasional-use gadgets can wait for a sale.
